What the label requires

The 48 points we check

We score every space on the things families notice. Reach the threshold and you carry the label. Fall short and you get a clear list of what would get you there.

Play & space

Something for kids to do, and room for a stroller between the tables, at the door and in the queue.

Safety & access

Door widths, steps, sharp corners, hot surfaces, what's within little arms' reach.

Food & care

Options for small appetites, high chairs, and a changing table that fathers can reach as well.

Team & welcome

How a family is received when they walk in. We watch this on the visit rather than asking about it.

The full scoring sheet stays with us; it's the work we do for you on site. These four areas are what it measures.

Questions we hear a lot

We're small. Is this for us?

Especially for you. The label started with independent cafés and ateliers, and most of the suggestions we make cost very little. What families notice is care, not square metres.

What if we don't reach the threshold on the first visit?

Then you leave the visit with a short list of what would get you there. Many partners fix it within weeks and get the label on the second look, with no extra visit fee.

How long does the label last?

One year. We check in before renewal, and your listing stays live for as long as the standards do.

Do you certify places outside Bern and Basel?

Yes. We've started in Zürich too, and we travel for the right partners. Write to us and we'll tell you honestly how soon we can visit.

Can we also get a PlayBox?

Of course. Many partners combine both: the label for the standard, the PlayBox for the play corner. Details on the PlayBox page.
