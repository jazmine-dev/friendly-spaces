Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Who we are

Friendly Spaces, Bern, Switzerland. Contact: hello@friendlyspaces.ch. This policy covers the website friendlyspaces.ch and the Friendly Spaces app.

What we collect, and why

  • Forms. When you contact us, nominate a place or sign up for news, we receive what you type (name, email, message) so we can reply or send you the newsletter. Submissions are processed by Netlify (Netlify, Inc.) on our behalf and forwarded to our mailbox.
  • Website analytics. friendlyspaces.ch uses privacy-friendly, cookie-free analytics to count visits. No personal profiles are built.
  • App and map analytics. The app and the embedded map use Google Analytics to understand which features and places are used. IP addresses are anonymised. You can use the map without accepting analytics by using your browser's tracking protection.
  • Location. The app only uses your location if you allow it, to show places near you. It is never stored by us.
  • Hosting. The website is served by Cloudflare; the app's data by Netlify. Both process technical connection data (such as IP addresses) to deliver the service securely.

What we don't do

We don't sell data, we don't run advertising networks, and we don't share your details with partner venues without asking you.

Your rights

You can ask us at any time what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, and unsubscribe from the newsletter with the link in every email. Write to hello@friendlyspaces.ch.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date.
