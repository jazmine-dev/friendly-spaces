What the label requires

Four areas, 48 points

We score every space on the things families notice. Once you pass the audit, you receive the label. If there are improvements to be made, we give you ideas on how to get there.

Questions we hear a lot

What if we don't pass on the first visit?

You get a short list of what would get you there. Places usually fix it within weeks and receive the label.

How long does the label last?

One year. We check in before renewal, and your listing stays live for as long as you're part of the network.

Can we also get a Playbox?

Yes. Several partners have both: the label for the standard, the Playbox for the play corner. Details on the Playbox page.
