What the label requires

Four areas, 48 points

We score every space on the things families notice. Reach the threshold and you carry the label. Fall short and you get a clear list of what would get you there.

Questions we hear a lot

We're small. Is this for us?

Especially for you. The label started with independent cafés and ateliers, and most of the suggestions we make cost very little. Families notice care long before they notice square metres.

What if we don't pass on the first visit?

You get a short list of what would get you there. Places usually fix it within weeks and get the label on a second look, with no extra visit fee.

How long does the label last?

One year. We check in before renewal, and your listing stays live for as long as the standard does.

Can we also get a PlayBox?

Yes. Several partners have both: the label for the standard, the PlayBox for the play corner. Details on the PlayBox page.
