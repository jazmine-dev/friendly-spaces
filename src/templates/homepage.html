What the label requires

48 points.
No secrets.

  • Play & space — something to do, and room to exist with a stroller
  • Safety & access — from door widths to what's within little arms' reach
  • Food & care — kids' options, high chairs, a changing table that exists
  • Team & welcome — the part no checklist can fake

Spaces are scored on our 48-point system and need to clear the threshold to carry the label. We check back regularly — the label is earned, then kept.
