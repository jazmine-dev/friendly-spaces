The pilot is over. Say hello to early bird.

September 2026 · Friendly Spaces

A year ago we started with a question and a handful of brave venues. Today the Friendly Spaces label is on windows in Bern, Basel and Zürich, the app is in both stores, and we've walked more play corners with a tape measure than we can count. The pilot phase has done its job. Here's what it taught us, and what changes now.

What the first partners showed us

The biggest lesson: family-friendliness is rarely about money. The changes that scored highest in our visits were almost always small — a high chair that's actually out, a corner with three toys instead of none, a changing table a father can find. Places that cared did those things within weeks. Places that didn't care were never going to, and that's fine; the label isn't for everyone.

The second lesson: parents use the map. Not to browse — to decide. The moment a place appeared as a pin, it started getting families who had planned to come, rather than families who happened by and hoped for the best.

What changes now

The pilot pricing was a thank-you for trusting us early. It's now replaced by early bird pricing: still deliberately accessible for an independent café, still including the on-site visit, the app listing, the map pin and the marketing material — and locked through the end of 2026.

The criteria are public now too. We publish what the 48 points measure, so a venue can see how close it is before we ever visit. The scoring itself stays with us; that's the work we do for you on site.

Where we're going

More cities, more kinds of spaces — museums and shops, not only cafés — and a PlayBox in every partner that wants one. If you run a place that already does it right, or one that could, write to us. And if you're a parent with a favourite, nominate it. That's how every pin so far has started.
