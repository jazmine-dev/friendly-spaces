What the label requires

The 48 points
we check

  • Play & space: something to do, and room for a stroller
  • Safety & access: door widths, steps, and what's within a toddler's reach
  • Food & care: kids' portions, high chairs, a changing table
  • Team & welcome: how a family is received when they walk in

Spaces are scored on our 48-point system and need to clear the threshold to carry the label. We come back regularly to check the standard is still there.
