Why now

Early means something here

Friendly Spaces is young and growing on purpose: every venue is visited, certified and re-checked by us personally. Sponsors who join now aren't a logo in a crowd — they're a founding supporter of Switzerland's family-friendly label, woven into the story as it's being written.

You reach parents at the exact moment they're deciding where to spend their day — and you reach them through a brand they trust to have their kids' backs.
